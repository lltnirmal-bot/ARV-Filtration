## Page 5: Services

### 1. CFD Flow Simulation Analysis
Using Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) modeling, we map gas velocity vectors inside ducting and inlet plenums to ensure uniform dust distribution. This eliminates high-velocity hotspots and increases bag life.

### 2. On-Site Leakage Detection (UV Test)
Fast diagnostic leak testing during planned shutdowns. We introduce fluorescent dye powder into the system and trace leaks using UV light to locate structural weld bypasses or torn bags.

### 3. Fabric Failure Analysis
Scientific root-cause diagnostic testing of spent or damaged filter bags. Our lab identifies chemical degradation (acid hydrolysis, alkaline attack) or physical abrasion, recommending the optimal fabric protective treatment.

### 4. Erection, Installation & Commissioning
Turnkey onsite execution. Our field engineers supervise system alignment, cage installation, tension adjustment, and control panel calibration to ensure trouble-free startup.

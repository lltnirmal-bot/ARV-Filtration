About ARV Filtration

We offer a comprehensive range of ready-to-install filter media crafted from virtually all types of synthetic and natural fibers. With process expertise extending over dry, wet, and liquid filtration industries, we are committed to delivering guaranteed performance regardless of your original equipment’s origin.

Why ARV Filtration? (Key Differentiators)

  • Tailored Fiber Chemistry: Filter Bag, Cage, Collecting plates & Emitting Electrode matched precisely to process load requirements.
  • Dimensional Accuracy: Cages and filter bags are engineered to fit together seamlessly, preventing wear-and-tear and structural leakages.
  • End-to-End Lifecycle Support: Complete design validation, simulation mapping, on-site commissioning, and troubleshooting assistance.

Machineries & Production Capabilities

Our plant contains specialized tooling to guarantee clean fabric sewing and metal tolerances:

  • Automatic Sewing Lines: Multi-needle lockstitch sewing setups for high seam burst strength.
  • Coating & Impregnation: Equipment for silicone oil, ePTFE membrane, and antistatic treatment.
  • Custom Metal Fabrication: Automated vertical wire cage welding, collecting plate rolling, and emitting electrode tooling.

Advanced Quality Testing Lab

We strictly test our media to verify parameters and check fabric endurance, ensuring you receive only defect-free, high-tenacity spares.

Weight Tester

Measures precise GSM (grams per sq. meter) of filter media.

Air Permeability

Calculates air flow resistance through filter fabrics.

Tensile Tester

Verifies fabric resistance under mechanical pulling forces.

Burst Tester

Measures multi-directional bursting pressure threshold.

Thickness Tester

Maintains uniformity of membrane layers down to microns.

Engineering

CFD Flow Simulation Analysis

Using Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) modeling, we map gas velocity vectors inside ducting and inlet plenums to ensure uniform dust distribution.

Troubleshoot

On-Site Leakage Detection (UV Test)

Fast diagnostic leak testing during planned shutdowns. We introduce fluorescent dye powder into the system and trace leaks using UV light to locate structural weld bypasses or torn bags.

  • Fast test execution during shutdown
  • Prevents outlet chimney opacity spikes
Lab Service
